Percutaneous nephrolithotomy (PCNL)

جلد کے راستے گردے کی پتھری نکالنے کا عمل (پی سی این ایل)

PCNL is a procedure used for selected kidney stones. It removes stones through a small access tract made through the skin into the kidney; it is not the right approach for every stone.

Discuss kidney stone care
Kidney stone procedure

How suitability is decided

The clinician reviews stone size and location, imaging, kidney function, infection risk, previous treatment, and overall health. Other options may be appropriate, and the expected benefits and risks of each should be compared before choosing a procedure.

Before the operation

Planning may include updated imaging, urine and blood tests, and a review of medicines, allergies, and anaesthesia needs. An active infection generally needs attention before an elective stone procedure; the clinical team will advise based on test results.

Procedure and recovery

During PCNL, a small passage is created from the back or side into the kidney so instruments can reach and remove or break up the stone. A temporary tube or stent may be used. Hospital stay and recovery vary, and follow-up imaging may be arranged to check for remaining fragments.

Possible risks

Bleeding, infection, injury to nearby structures, residual stone, or need for another procedure are among the risks the treating urologist may discuss. Seek urgent advice after discharge for fever, worsening pain, heavy bleeding, or difficulty passing urine.

Stone assessment

Bring prior scans and stone analysis results if available. A consultation can clarify whether PCNL or another approach merits discussion.

Care for children

Paediatric urology

بچوں کے یورولوجی مسائل

Urological concerns in children need assessment suited to the child's age, symptoms, development, and family circumstances. A parent or guardian can take part in planning the visit.

Request a paediatric consultation
Family-centred care

Concerns families may bring

Consultations may involve urinary infections, problems with urine flow or continence, undescended testis, hypospadias, or findings noted on prenatal or later imaging. These examples do not determine the diagnosis; a clinician needs to review the individual child.

Assessment with the family

The visit may include questions about the child's symptoms, growth, prior illnesses, and investigations. Depending on the concern, examination, urine testing, ultrasound, or other tests may be appropriate. The reason for each examination or test should be explained to the caregiver.

Planning care

Some conditions are observed over time, while others may need medication or an operation. Timing and follow-up depend on the specific finding. Parents can discuss the expected process, alternatives, and how to prepare a child for any recommended care.

Urgent symptoms

Seek prompt medical attention if a child is unable to urinate, has fever with urinary symptoms, severe pain, or appears significantly unwell.

Plan a family visit

Bring relevant reports, a current medicine list, and a caregiver's observations about symptoms or timing.

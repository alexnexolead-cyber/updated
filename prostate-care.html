Prostate care

Prostate symptoms and evaluation

پروسٹیٹ کی علامات اور معائنہ

Prostate conditions can affect urinary flow and bladder habits. Symptoms have several possible explanations, so evaluation should be based on your health and history.

Prostate care

Changes to mention

Let your clinician know about a weak or interrupted stream, difficulty starting, a sense that the bladder has not emptied, urgency, or getting up at night to urinate. Pain, fever, or blood in the urine may point to other problems that also need attention.

How prostate concerns are assessed

The discussion may cover how symptoms affect daily life, medicines, and family or medical history. Depending on your age and situation, examination, urine testing, flow measurement, imaging, or a prostate-specific antigen blood test may be considered. A PSA result alone does not establish a diagnosis.

Treatment discussions

Some symptoms can be monitored; others may call for medicine or a procedure. The choice depends on the underlying cause, symptom burden, test results, and your preferences. Ask about potential benefits, side effects, alternatives, and review plans before deciding.

When symptoms need prompt review

Inability to urinate, fever or chills, severe pain, or visible blood should not be left to a routine follow-up appointment.

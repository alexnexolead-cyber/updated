Uro-oncology

Urological cancer assessment

یورو آنکولوجی

Uro-oncology focuses on cancers involving organs of the urinary tract and male reproductive system. A consultation can help review findings and explain possible next steps.

Request an appointment
Individualized assessment

Reviewing a concern or diagnosis

Appointments may concern a finding in the kidney, bladder, prostate, or testicle, or a diagnosis already made elsewhere. Bring scan reports, biopsy or pathology results, and previous treatment details so the information can be reviewed together.

Tests and staging

Further imaging, laboratory tests, examination, or tissue sampling may be considered depending on what is already known. The purpose is to understand the diagnosis and extent of disease before discussing a plan; a single test result may not provide the whole picture.

Discussing treatment pathways

Options vary by cancer type, stage, and individual health. Care may involve monitoring, surgery, medicines, or input from other specialists. Your clinician can explain the reason for each option, potential effects, and whether a multidisciplinary review is appropriate.

Support between visits

Write down questions and bring someone you trust if helpful. Ask whom to contact if symptoms change or if you need help understanding results after the appointment.

Bring your reports

Recent imaging and pathology documents help make a cancer-care discussion more specific to your situation.

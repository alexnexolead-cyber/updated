Robotic urological surgery

روبوٹک یورولوجیکل سرجری

Robot-assisted surgery is a technique in which the surgeon controls instruments from a console. The system does not operate independently, and the approach is relevant only for selected procedures.

Discuss a surgical opinion
Surgical care

When this approach may be considered

Whether a robot-assisted operation is appropriate depends on the condition being treated, anatomy, previous treatment, overall health, and available expertise and facilities. It is not automatically preferable to open, laparoscopic, endoscopic, or non-surgical care.

Planning and shared decisions

Before surgery, the clinician reviews test results and explains the intended procedure, alternatives, possible complications, expected hospital care, and follow-up. Patients should ask how the proposed technique relates to their particular diagnosis.

Recovery varies

Recovery instructions depend on the operation and individual health. Your care team can describe likely activity restrictions, wound care, medicines, and warning signs after the procedure rather than relying on a general timeline.

Talk through your options

Bring imaging reports, pathology results if applicable, and a list of medicines to a consultation.

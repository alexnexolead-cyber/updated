Penile implant procedures

پینائل امپلانٹ کے طریقۂ کار

A penile implant is a surgical option that may be discussed for some people with persistent erectile dysfunction. It requires a careful consultation about goals, alternatives, and long-term considerations.

Request a private consultation
Implant consultation

Is an implant appropriate?

The decision follows review of the cause of erectile dysfunction, general health, previous treatments, and personal preferences. Other medical and non-surgical options may be considered first or may remain more appropriate for an individual patient.

What the consultation covers

The clinician can explain implant types, the operation, anaesthesia, expected use of the device, and practical recovery needs. Potential complications include infection, pain, device problems, or a need for further surgery; specific risks vary by patient.

After the procedure

Follow-up is needed to check healing and provide guidance about using the device. Timing and activity advice are determined by the treating team. The procedure does not address every cause of sexual difficulty, so expectations should be discussed openly beforehand.

Reconstructive urology

باز تعمیری یورولوجی

Reconstructive urology addresses selected problems in the urinary tract that affect its structure or function. Care planning starts with understanding the original cause and previous treatment.

Reconstructive care

Conditions that may need review

Assessment can be relevant for urethral narrowing, urinary leakage, or structural changes after injury or earlier procedures. Each problem has different causes and options, and not every concern requires reconstructive surgery.

Building a clear picture

Bring reports from earlier operations, catheter use, imaging, and any prior endoscopy if available. Evaluation may involve urine tests, imaging, or examination of the urinary passage to locate and understand the problem.

Discussing possible repair

If an operation is being considered, the consultation should cover the intended change, other approaches, possible effects on urinary function, risks, recovery needs, and how results will be followed. A second opinion can be useful for complex decisions.

Prepare for your visit

Past operative notes and test reports can help avoid repeating investigations and support a focused discussion.

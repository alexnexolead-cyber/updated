Bladder symptoms and care

Bladder care

Symptoms worth discussing

Tell the clinician if you have to urinate unusually often, wake repeatedly at night, feel sudden urgency, leak urine, have difficulty emptying, or notice discomfort. Blood in the urine should be assessed even if it happens once.

What an assessment may include

Questions about fluid intake, medicines, prior infections, and when symptoms occur can help identify patterns. Depending on the concern, evaluation may include a urine test, examination, bladder scan after urination, or other imaging.

Choosing a care plan

Management varies with the cause. Options can include changes to daily habits, treatment of an identified infection or other condition, medication, or a procedure. Your clinician can explain why a proposed option fits your findings and what follow-up is needed.

Get urgent help for severe symptoms

Seek prompt medical help if you cannot pass urine, have visible blood with clots, develop fever with urinary symptoms, or feel acutely unwell.

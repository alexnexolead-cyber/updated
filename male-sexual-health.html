Male sexual health consultation

Sexual health concerns are common reasons to seek medical advice. A private, respectful conversation can help identify possible causes and sensible next steps.

Request an appointment
Private consultation

Concerns you can discuss

Patients may seek advice about erectile difficulties, changes in ejaculation, or other changes in sexual function. These concerns can relate to general health, medicines, emotional wellbeing, or more than one factor.

What to expect from evaluation

The clinician may ask when the change began, whether it is consistent, what medicines you take, and whether you have other health conditions. Examination or tests can be suggested when they are relevant; you can ask why a test is recommended and what its result may clarify.

Options are not the same for everyone

Depending on the assessment, discussion can include changes to health habits, medication review, medical treatment, or procedural options. Penile implants are a separate surgical consideration for selected patients and are not suitable for everyone.

Privacy and safety

Please use the appointment form only to request a visit; it is not an emergency service. Avoid entering intimate health details in the online form. Contact the clinic directly if you need to discuss sensitive information.
